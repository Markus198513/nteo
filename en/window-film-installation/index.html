01 / CONSULTATION · MEASUREMENT · INSTALLATION

Window film solutions for solar control, privacy and design

Windows, shopfronts and glass partitions influence daylight, comfort and privacy. Specialist films can enhance existing glazing without replacing the glass units. Depending on the brief, a film can help manage solar impact, provide daytime privacy, create a frosted or decorative finish, switch between clear and opaque states, or help retain fragments if glass is damaged. The right specification depends on the glass type, location and intended use of the space.

02 / OUR SERVICES

Specialist film solutions for windows, shopfronts and glass

Explore six film solution categories for homes, offices, shops, hotels and other commercial properties. Selection and installation are planned around the characteristics of the existing glazing.

01 / MIRROR SOLAR FILM

Mirror Solar Control Film

Mirror films can help reduce solar heat impact and provide added daytime privacy. Suitable for house and villa windows, panoramic glazing, offices and commercial premises. The reflective effect depends on the difference in exterior and interior light; privacy may reduce at night when interior lights are on.

02 / CLEAR SOLAR CONTROL FILM

Clear Solar Control Film

A solution for spaces where maintaining the natural appearance of glass, clear views and daylight is important while reducing the impact of solar radiation. Suitable for panoramic windows, terraces, shopfronts, showrooms and offices where the appearance of the glazing should remain largely unchanged.

03 / DECORATIVE & FROSTED FILM

Decorative & Frosted Film

Frosted and decorative finishes help divide spaces, add privacy and enhance glass surfaces. Options include frosted areas, stripes, geometric compositions and custom graphics for offices, salons, restaurants, retail spaces and private interiors.

04 / SMART FILM / PDLC

Switchable Smart Film

Smart Film switches glass between transparent and opaque states using electrical control. It is suited to meeting rooms, executive offices, glass partitions and selected residential interiors. Each project requires an assessment of the glazing, dimensions, electrical connection and operating conditions.

05 / SAFETY & SECURITY FILM

Safety & Security Film

Safety films are designed to help hold fragments together if glass is damaged and reduce the risk of shards scattering. Suitable for homes, offices, shops and public-facing premises. Performance depends on the specific film, glass type and installation method; film does not make glass unbreakable.

06 / CUSTOM GLASS FILM SOLUTIONS

Custom Glass Film Solutions

We consider tailored solutions for glass surfaces, including combinations of clear and frosted areas, custom decorative compositions, glass door and shopfront treatments, space zoning and branded designs for commercial interiors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you install film on existing windows?

Yes. NTEO selects a suitable solution and installs it on existing glass surfaces after checking compatibility with the glazing.

Which areas of Cyprus do you cover?

Our main service areas are Limassol, Nicosia, Paphos and Larnaca. Visits to Ayia Napa, Protaras and other areas can be arranged by agreement.

Can you install film on shopfronts or office partitions?

Yes. We work with shopfront glazing, glass doors, office partitions, panoramic windows and other suitable glass surfaces.

How do I choose between mirror, clear solar and frosted film?

The right option depends on your goals: solar control, daytime privacy, visibility or decorative treatment. We help assess the property and choose an appropriate solution.
