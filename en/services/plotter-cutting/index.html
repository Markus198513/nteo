Vinyl Plotter Cutting: Lettering, Logos and Custom Decals

We cut self-adhesive film to the exact outline of supplied or prepared artwork. Plotter cutting is ideal for lettering, logos, icons, labels, stencils and decorative graphics without a printed background.

Materials and technologies

01

Monomeric vinyl

For short-term applications on flat surfaces.

02

Polymeric vinyl

For longer-term applications and improved dimensional stability.

03

Cast vinyl

For complex surfaces and applications requiring greater conformability.

04

Application tape

For transferring cut graphics from the liner to the final surface.

How a project is delivered

  1. 01

    Prepare vector artwork and check all cut paths

  2. 02

    Choose film by colour, surface and intended service life

  3. 03

    Cut, weed excess vinyl and apply transfer tape

  4. 04

    Pack with application guidance or arrange installation

Specification note: the final material and production method are selected after reviewing dimensions, surface conditions, installation location and expected use. Photos and approximate measurements are enough to start a discussion.

Frequently asked questions

Can you recommend more than one finish or material?

Yes. After reviewing the brief, we can discuss suitable options based on appearance, material and installation method.

What do you need for an initial estimate?

Send photos of the site, approximate dimensions, the location and a short description of the requirement. Include a logo or artwork if available.

Can installation be arranged across Cyprus?

Site visits and installation are confirmed individually based on the address, scope and type of work.
