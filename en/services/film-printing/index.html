A considered approach to every brief

Printed vinyl is suited to full-colour graphics on glass and smooth surfaces. Material choice depends on the substrate, intended duration, exposure conditions and whether a protective laminate is needed.

  • Protective laminated graphics
  • Window and wall films
  • Panels prepared for installation
02

Materials & components

  • Self-adhesive vinyl
  • Protective laminate
  • Films for smooth surfaces
  • Short- and long-term media options
