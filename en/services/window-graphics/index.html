Window Graphics & Glass Branding: Films, Privacy and Visual Communication

Glass can become a brand touchpoint, a wayfinding surface and a privacy solution. We select the right application for each space — from subtle manifestation bands to full frosted coverage, cut lettering and printed window campaigns.

Options, finishes and application examples

The gallery below shows illustrative concepts of how this service can be applied in real environments. These are visual examples, not claims of completed NTEO projects. The final specification depends on the site, materials and technical conditions.

Full frosted coverage

Creates a soft, translucent finish and screens direct views while allowing daylight through. Suitable for meeting rooms, clinics and glass partitions.

Frosted bands and manifestation

Horizontal bands and visual markers make clear glass easier to notice and define the edges of glazed partitions.

Cut logos and lettering

Printed graphics on clear film

Perforated One Way Vision

Perforated printed film for shopfronts. Graphics face outward while some outward visibility from inside can remain, depending on lighting.

Solar-control and tinted films

Selected films can reduce glare and solar heat gain. Suitability depends on the glazing and the intended application.

How a project is delivered

  1. 01

    Measure the glazing, photograph the area and check the surface

  2. 02

    Select film type, opacity and graphic layout

  3. 03

    Prepare artwork around frames, handles, joints and clear zones

  4. 04

    Produce, install and inspect the finished graphics

Specification note: the final material and production method are selected after reviewing dimensions, surface conditions, installation location and expected use. Photos and approximate measurements are enough to start a discussion.

Frequently asked questions

Can you recommend more than one finish or material?

Yes. After reviewing the brief, we can discuss suitable options based on appearance, material and installation method.

What do you need for an initial estimate?

Send photos of the site, approximate dimensions, the location and a short description of the requirement. Include a logo or artwork if available.

Can installation be arranged across Cyprus?

Site visits and installation are confirmed individually based on the address, scope and type of work.
