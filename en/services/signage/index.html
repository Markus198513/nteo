Frequently asked questions

What do you need for an initial project review?

Send the approximate dimensions or photos of the location, a short description of the brief and your logo or artwork if available. We can then discuss a suitable approach.

Projects can be discussed in Limassol, Nicosia, Paphos, Larnaca and other areas of Cyprus. Site visits and installation are confirmed individually.
