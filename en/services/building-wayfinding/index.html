Building Wayfinding: Directional Signs, Room IDs and Sign Systems

A clear wayfinding system helps visitors identify entrances, reach the right floor and find specific rooms. We create coordinated sign families for residential developments, offices, clinics, hotels and public spaces.

How a project is delivered

  1. 01

    Review the building, routes and common visitor questions

  2. 02

    Create the wayfinding map, message hierarchy and pictogram system

  3. 03

    Select formats, materials, fixings and artwork

  4. 04

    Produce, install and check legibility on site

Specification note: the final material and production method are selected after reviewing dimensions, surface conditions, installation location and expected use. Photos and approximate measurements are enough to start a discussion.

Frequently asked questions

Can you recommend more than one finish or material?

Yes. After reviewing the brief, we can discuss suitable options based on appearance, material and installation method.

What do you need for an initial estimate?

Send photos of the site, approximate dimensions, the location and a short description of the requirement. Include a logo or artwork if available.

Can installation be arranged across Cyprus?

Site visits and installation are confirmed individually based on the address, scope and type of work.
