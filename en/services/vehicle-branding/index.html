NTEO · VEHICLE BRANDING TECHNOLOGIES

Vehicle branding — from the first concept to finished graphics

We design graphics for cars, vans, minibuses and commercial fleets. The method is selected according to the design, budget, vehicle geometry and expected service conditions. Explore the main application methods and coverage options before choosing a solution.

01 / Ways to apply vehicle graphics

PRINT · LAMINATION · INSTALLATION

Full-colour print on film

Photographs, complex gradients, illustrations and brand graphics are printed on self-adhesive film using a wide-format printer. For exterior vehicle graphics, a compatible clear overlaminate is normally used to help protect the print before installation. Ideal for bold advertising panels and full graphic projects.

COLOURED FILM · VECTOR · APPLICATION

Plotter cutting (cut vinyl)

Logos, company names, phone numbers, web addresses and simple vector shapes are cut from pre-coloured vinyl rolls, such as suitable Oracal 641 or 551 series. Excess film is weeded away and the finished elements are transferred to the vehicle with application tape. A fast, economical option for contact details and clean branding.

PRINT + CUT VINYL

Combined method

Key elements such as logos, lettering and contact details are made from coloured cut vinyl, while photographs, complex gradients and decorative artwork are printed separately. This approach combines crisp brand elements with full-colour imagery where it adds value.

02 / Vehicle branding by coverage area

The right coverage depends on how the vehicle is used and where it is seen: sometimes a logo and phone number are enough, while other projects need a large advertising surface or a consistent fleet identity.

Partial branding

Graphics are placed on doors, the bonnet, rear window or selected body panels. Suitable for taxis, delivery vehicles, service cars and small businesses that need visible contact details without covering the entire vehicle.

CONCEPT VISUAL

Side-panel wrap

Graphics cover most of the side surface of a van or bus. Suitable polymeric films may be used on appropriate flat panels; recessed areas and complex curves need individual assessment. A practical format for vehicles working around the city every day.

CONCEPT VISUAL

Full vehicle branding

Most or nearly all of the vehicle is covered with one coordinated graphic system. For complex curves, bumpers, recesses and body channels, premium cast films are commonly considered where the film specification supports the application.

03 / Vehicle films and expected service life

Film type affects which body areas it can be used on and how it performs over time. The periods below are indicative comparison ranges, not guarantees: actual life depends on the manufacturer, product series, preparation, installation, climate and care.

01 · ECONOMY

Calendered film

For short-term graphics and mainly flat surfaces. Shrinkage or edge lifting may occur on complex contours.

Indicative: 6–12 months
02 · MID-RANGE

Polymeric film

A middle option for selected commercial applications and moderately curved areas. Always check the specific product data sheet.

Indicative: 2–3 years
03 · PREMIUM

Cast film

A more conformable material for complex curves, body channels, recesses and bumpers when installed correctly.

Indicative: 5–7 years
Overlamination for printed graphics

Exterior full-colour vehicle prints are normally paired with a compatible protective overlaminate. It helps reduce abrasion, UV exposure and wear from washing. Cut graphics made from pre-coloured vinyl generally do not require lamination.

Vehicle preparation

The vehicle is washed, road tar and contaminants are removed, and the surface is fully dried and cleaned with a suitable degreasing product. Paint condition, corrosion and previous repairs should be checked before installation.

Body geometry

Handles, trims, deep channels, panel joins and bumpers need special attention. Film and installation methods must follow the manufacturer's limits for stretching and application.

Use and maintenance

Service life is affected by sunlight, temperature, washing frequency and method, chemicals and storage. Follow the care instructions supplied for the selected film and laminate.

Vehicle branding FAQs

Should I choose printed graphics or plotter-cut vinyl?

Printing is suitable for photographs, complex gradients and multi-colour artwork. Plotter-cut vinyl works well for logos, lettering and simple vector shapes cut from coloured film. Many commercial vehicle designs combine both methods.

Does printed vehicle graphics need lamination?

Exterior printed graphics are commonly specified with a compatible protective laminate to help reduce wear from washing and everyday use. Compatibility should be checked against the film manufacturer’s recommendations.

Can I brand only the doors or rear of my vehicle?

Yes. Partial branding can place logos and contact details on selected panels, doors or suitable glazed areas. The layout should account for visibility, body lines and legibility.

How long will vehicle vinyl last?

Service life depends on the exact film series, surface preparation, installation, sun exposure, washing and vehicle storage. The ranges on this page are indicative comparisons, not a guarantee for every product.

What should I send for an initial estimate?

Send photos of the vehicle from several sides, its make and model, the approximate coverage area, your logo or artwork and a short description of the intended result. This helps us suggest a suitable method and material.
