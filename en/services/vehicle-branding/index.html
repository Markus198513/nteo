NTEO · VEHICLE BRANDING TECHNOLOGIES

Vehicle graphics are a considered system, not just a sticker

We develop branding for cars, vans and commercial fleets. Every layout considers body shape, doors, handles, panel lines, glazed areas and how the message will read in motion. The application method is selected around the design, budget and intended service life.

Ways to apply advertising to a vehicle

Different briefs call for different technologies. Full-colour printing reproduces photographs and complex artwork; plotter-cut lettering is ideal for logos and concise messages; the combined method brings both together.

01 · Full-colour printed vinyl

Photographs, gradients, illustrations and branded artwork are printed onto self-adhesive vinyl. For exterior use, printed graphics are commonly protected with a compatible laminate before being prepared and applied to the vehicle. Suitable for bold side graphics and complete promotional wraps.

PRINT · LAMINATE · INSTALL

02 · Plotter cutting and application

Logos, names, phone numbers and simple vector shapes are cut from pre-coloured roll vinyl — for example, suitable Oracal 641 or 551 series. The cut elements are transferred to the vehicle using application tape. A practical option for clean branding and contact details.

COLOURED VINYL · VECTOR · APPLICATION

03 · Combined method

Core elements such as logos, names and contact details are produced as cut graphics, while photographs, complex gradients and decorative panels are printed separately. This balances visual impact, clean lettering and budget control.

PRINT + CUT VINYL

Vehicle branding by coverage area

Coverage is not only a budget decision. It should reflect where the vehicle operates, which sides are most visible and how much information the audience can read at a glance.

Partial branding

Logos and contact details on doors, rear glass or selected body panels. Suitable for service vehicles, delivery cars, taxis and small businesses that need clear identification without covering the whole vehicle.

ILLUSTRATIVE CONCEPT

Side-panel graphics

Graphics cover a large part of a van or bus side. More economical materials may be suitable for appropriate flat panels; curves, recesses and complex areas need separate assessment.

ILLUSTRATIVE CONCEPT

Full vehicle branding

Most or nearly all of the body is covered with one coordinated graphic system. Complex curves, bumpers and recesses typically call for premium cast films specified for the application.

ILLUSTRATIVE CONCEPT

Vehicle films: choosing the right material

Price per roll is only one factor. Consider body geometry, recess depth, campaign duration, washing frequency, sun exposure and the technical recommendations for the specific film series.

01 · ECONOMY

Calendered films

A practical choice for short-term applications and flatter vehicle panels. They may be unsuitable for deep recesses and complex curves, where shrinkage or edge lifting can occur. A 6–12 month range is an indicative example for selected products and conditions, not a guarantee.

02 · MID-RANGE

Polymeric films

An intermediate option for many commercial applications and moderately shaped surfaces. Selection depends on the technical data for the specific series and its permitted application conditions. A 2–3 year range is indicative and varies with use.

03 · PREMIUM

Cast films

More conformable films for complex body contours, edges and bumpers. When correctly specified, prepared and installed, they are better suited to shaped surfaces. Some product ranges may offer an indicative 5–7 year service life; this is not a universal warranty.

04 · PRINT PROTECTION

Protective laminate

A compatible gloss or matte laminate helps protect the printed layer against abrasion, washing, UV exposure and environmental wear. The laminate should be selected together with the printable film.

Technical details that influence the result

01

Laminating printed graphics

Most exterior printed vehicle graphics use a compatible protective laminate. It helps reduce image wear, but does not replace correct film selection or professional installation. Pre-coloured cut vinyl lettering generally does not require print lamination.

02

Vehicle preparation

The vehicle is washed, cleaned of tar and contaminants, thoroughly dried and degreased with a suitable product. Paint condition, corrosion and previous repairs should be assessed before wrapping.

03

Body geometry and risk areas

Handles, trims, deep recesses, panel joints and bumpers require careful layout and suitable film. Not every vinyl can be safely stretched over complex contours.

04

Use and maintenance

Service life is affected by sun, temperature, washing, chemicals and vehicle storage. After installation, follow the film manufacturer’s and installer’s guidance for initial care and washing.

Vehicle branding FAQs

Should I choose printed graphics or plotter-cut vinyl?

Printing is suitable for photographs, complex gradients and multi-colour artwork. Plotter-cut vinyl works well for logos, lettering and simple vector shapes cut from coloured film. Many commercial vehicle designs combine both methods.

Does printed vehicle graphics need lamination?

Exterior printed graphics are commonly specified with a compatible protective laminate to help reduce wear from washing and everyday use. Compatibility should be checked against the film manufacturer’s recommendations.

Can I brand only the doors or rear of my vehicle?

Yes. Partial branding can place logos and contact details on selected panels, doors or suitable glazed areas. The layout should account for visibility, body lines and legibility.

How long will vehicle vinyl last?

Service life depends on the exact film series, surface preparation, installation, sun exposure, washing and vehicle storage. The ranges on this page are indicative comparisons, not a guarantee for every product.

What should I send for an initial estimate?

Send photos of the vehicle from several sides, its make and model, the approximate coverage area, your logo or artwork and a short description of the intended result. This helps us suggest a suitable method and material.
