OVERVIEW

A clear brief makes a better result.

Scope and coordination

The service begins with a clear brief: what needs to be produced, where it will be used, what dimensions and finish are expected, and what information is already available. Scope is confirmed before work proceeds.

Project stages

Depending on the project, stages may include consultation, measurements, artwork preparation, visual approval, material selection, production coordination and installation planning. Not every project requires every stage.

Information to share

Send reference images, site photographs, dimensions, quantity, location and deadline. NTEO will review the brief and clarify the next practical step.
