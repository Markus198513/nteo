NTEO / CYPRUS

Visual solutions for businesses in Cyprus

NTEO is a full-service advertising and production company in Cyprus, bringing together design, print, wide-format graphics, outdoor and interior advertising, illuminated and non-illuminated signs, vinyl plotter cutting, vehicle branding, window and façade graphics, information plaques and building wayfinding. We create advertising products for shops, restaurants, offices, business centres, construction companies, hotels and resorts. Production methods, materials and finishes are selected for each project and architectural setting. We welcome enquiries from Limassol, Paphos, Nicosia, Larnaca, Ayia Napa, Protaras and other areas across Cyprus. Delivery and installation are arranged individually.
