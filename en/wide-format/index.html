01 / APPLICATIONS

WHERE VINYL PRINTING WORKS

Self-adhesive vinyl makes it possible to apply full-colour images, photography, brand graphics and promotional messages to a wide range of smooth surfaces.

Windows & shopfronts

Promotional campaigns, seasonal graphics, images and branded designs applied to glass surfaces.

Vehicles & transport

Full-colour vinyl graphics for cars, vans, commercial vehicles and business fleets.

Interiors & walls

Photo murals and decorative graphics for offices, cafés, salons, retail and public spaces.

Signage & advertising

Printed graphics for advertising panels, information displays and commercial façades.

Promotions & campaigns

Seasonal offers, sales campaigns and temporary graphics for retail spaces.

Business branding

Coordinated visual graphics for commercial spaces, façades, windows and other surfaces.

Construction sites & hoardings

Full-colour vinyl graphics applied directly to smooth construction hoarding panels, including property visuals and developer information.

Floor graphics & wayfinding

Promotional graphics, directional signs and wayfinding elements applied to floors in malls, shops and commercial spaces.

Exhibitions & events

Printed graphics for exhibition stands, branded zones, photo backdrops, conferences and presentations.

02 / MATERIALS

The right vinyl for the job

Finish, surface and operating conditions all affect material choice. We can recommend a suitable option once we understand your project.

Gloss vinyl

Bright, saturated finish for promotional graphics and images.

Matt vinyl

A more subtle finish with reduced reflections for interiors and windows.

Perforated window film

For suitable glass applications where outward visibility is important.

Cast & calendered vinyl

Material choice depends on the surface, shape, service life and installation conditions.
