07 / FAQ

RESIDENTIAL SIGNAGE — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can you produce floor numbers and apartment plaques in different materials?

Yes. Depending on the design and installation surface, we can discuss acrylic, metal, aluminium and PVC options, including flat or dimensional lettering.

Can you make exterior signs for residential blocks?

Yes. Block identifiers, entrance numbers and building directories can be designed for exterior placement. The material and fixing method are selected for the location and substrate.

Can you create a complete wayfinding system for a complex?

We can coordinate outdoor directories, directional signs, building identifiers, floor and apartment numbering, parking signs and shared-facility information as one project.

Do you make pool rules and depth signs?

Yes. We can manufacture signs showing pool depth, permitted and prohibited activities and other agreed rules. Final wording and safety requirements should be approved by the property manager or responsible specialist.

Can you produce parking numbers and floor stencils?

We can discuss bay numbering, wall signs, arrows, zone identifiers and stencils for suitable parking surfaces. The marking method depends on the floor finish and expected use.

Do you prepare official evacuation plans?

We manufacture evacuation plan boards and related signs using plans and content approved by the responsible fire-safety or project specialist. We do not replace the specialist design or approval process.

Which areas of Cyprus do you cover?

We accept enquiries from Limassol, Paphos, Larnaca, Nicosia, Ayia Napa, Protaras and surrounding communities. Delivery and installation availability are confirmed for each project.

RESIDENTIAL COMPLEX SIGNAGE, APARTMENT NUMBERING & WAYFINDING IN CYPRUS

NTEO provides residential complex signage in Cyprus for property developers, apartment owners, property management companies, architects and contractors. Our service covers the practical visual elements used every day by residents and visitors: floor and level numbers, apartment door plaques, exterior block identifiers, entrance signs, building directories and directional wayfinding.

For shared facilities, we manufacture resident information boards, house-rule panels, pool depth and safety signs, parking bay numbers, floor and wall markings, evacuation plan boards and emergency-direction signs.
