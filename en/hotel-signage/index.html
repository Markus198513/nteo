05 / FAQ

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Can you create a complete signage package for a hotel?

Yes. We can plan a coordinated set of room numbers, door signs, guest-area signs and service signage. The scope is defined around the property, its design standards and operational needs.

Which materials and finishes are available?

Depending on the application, options may include acrylic, aluminium, stainless steel, brass-look finishes, PVC, composite and wood. Final selection depends on location, exposure and maintenance requirements.

Can signs be customised to match our hotel brand?

Yes. Typography, colours, dimensions, materials and finishes can be developed to fit the hotel’s visual identity and interior concept.

Do you provide installation in Cyprus?

Installation can be discussed for Limassol, Paphos, Nicosia, Larnaca, Ayia Napa, Protaras and other areas. Availability and cost depend on the address and project scope.
