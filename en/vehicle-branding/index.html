VEHICLE WRAPPING & BRANDING MADE TO ORDER

SERVING · CYPRUS · PAPHOS · LIMASSOL · LARNACA · NICOSIA

Vehicle wrapping by NTEO is more than a new look — it is a strategic business decision. Our vinyl graphics help your message travel further, create lasting impressions and reach audiences that traditional advertising may not.

Whether you are a local service provider, a delivery company or a national brand, vehicle wrapping is one of the most cost-effective ways to advertise in Cyprus’s busy, dynamic environment. With bold graphics, quality materials and professional installation, we turn your vehicle into an eye-catching mobile advertising space that works for your business every day.

01 / NTEO SERVICES

VEHICLE WRAPPING, BRANDING & VINYL GRAPHICS

Choose the right format for your vehicle: from full-body vinyl wraps to plotter-cut lettering and printed graphics on vehicle-grade film.

01 / FULL VEHICLE WRAPPING

Full Vehicle Wrapping

Full-body vinyl wrapping with a coordinated brand colour scheme and large-format advertising graphics. We plan the design around the vehicle shape and the goals of your project.

02 / PARTIAL VEHICLE WRAPPING

Partial Vehicle Wrapping

Vinyl graphics applied to selected body panels, doors, bonnet or rear sections. A practical option for logos, contact details and distinctive brand graphics without a full wrap.

03 / VAN BRANDING

Van Branding

Branding for delivery vans, service vehicles and commercial transport. We prepare artwork for the vehicle body, produce the vinyl graphics and arrange professional installation.

04 / FLEET BRANDING

Fleet Branding

A consistent visual identity across multiple company vehicles. We coordinate layouts, colours, logos and graphic placement so your fleet looks recognisable across Cyprus.

05 / CAR STICKERS & VINYL LETTERING

Car Stickers & Vinyl Lettering

Plotter-cut vehicle decals, logos, phone numbers, website addresses and lettering made from coloured vinyl. We apply the cut graphics using application tape.

06 / PRINTED VEHICLE GRAPHICS

Printed Vehicle Graphics

Full-colour graphics printed on self-adhesive films for vehicle applications. We prepare artwork, select suitable media and add compatible laminate where required before installation.

07 / FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I order just a logo or lettering instead of a full wrap?

Yes. Partial branding can include a logo, phone number, website, list of services and selected graphic elements.

Do you provide design services?

We can prepare artwork based on your brand identity or adapt files you provide. The scope is agreed before production.

Can you brand several vehicles?

Yes. We can discuss a consistent design for a fleet, the number of vehicles and model-specific layouts.

How much does vehicle branding cost?

Pricing depends on the vehicle model and dimensions, coverage area, film type, artwork complexity and installation requirements. Send us a vehicle photo and project brief for an estimate.

Do you work outside Limassol?

Yes, we consider projects across Cyprus. Travel and installation arrangements depend on the location and project scope.
