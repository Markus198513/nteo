OVERVIEW

A clear brief makes a better result.

Start with the purpose

Define the audience, the message and the action the sign or printed item should support. A clear brief helps avoid unnecessary revisions and keeps design decisions connected to the real use case.

Check the site conditions

Review dimensions, surfaces, access, lighting and environmental exposure before choosing a material or production method. Photographs and measurements are useful at the first discussion stage.

Plan artwork and installation

Confirm final dimensions, artwork format, colour expectations, fixing method and who is responsible for installation. Allow time for proof approval before production begins.
