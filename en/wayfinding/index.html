01 / WAYFINDING SOLUTIONS

ELEMENTS OF ONE WAYFINDING SYSTEM

Need clear, professional wayfinding for an office, residential development, hotel or commercial building in Cyprus? NTEO designs, manufactures and installs complete building signage systems — entrance totems, information stands, directories, floor maps, directional signs, door plaques, room numbers, WC signs, accessibility and safety signage. We help select suitable materials, formats and finishes to match your architecture, bringing every sign together in one consistent visual system that makes buildings easier to navigate. Wayfinding signage design, production and installation in Limassol, Paphos, Nicosia, Larnaca and across Cyprus, subject to project requirements.

05 / FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can NTEO create a complete wayfinding system for a building?

Yes. We can coordinate directories, directional signs, room identification and other agreed elements under one visual concept. The scope is defined after reviewing the building and project requirements.

Can existing signs be updated when tenants change?

Modular and replaceable sign formats can be considered where information is expected to change. The best construction depends on the sign type and location.

Which materials are available?

Depending on the application, options may include acrylic, aluminium, other metals, PVC, composite panels, wood and printed or engraved finishes.

Do you install signage throughout Cyprus?

Installation can be discussed for Limassol, Paphos, Nicosia, Larnaca and other areas. Availability and cost depend on the site address, access and project scope.

Can you produce tactile and safety signs?

These can be included in the project brief. Safety and accessibility signs must be specified and checked against the applicable requirements and standards for the particular building.
