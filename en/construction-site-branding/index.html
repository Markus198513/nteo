CONSTRUCTION SITE BRANDING AND HOARDING IN CYPRUS

Construction hoarding is more than a boundary around a work area. It is a large visible surface that can inform passers-by, present a future development and build recognition for the developer. NTEO designs and produces visual branding for construction sites, from individual banners and information panels to coordinated perimeter graphics.

We help bring architectural visualisations, project identity, development information and practical site signage together in one consistent system. Formats and materials are selected according to fence dimensions, installation conditions, project duration and the character of the site.

07 / FAQ

CONSTRUCTION SITE BRANDING — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What types of construction fencing can be branded?

We can discuss modular temporary fences, solid perimeter panels, individual fence sections, scaffold mesh and other surfaces suitable for the selected material and fixing method.

Can you print architectural visualisations of the future building?

Yes. Artwork can include supplied architectural renders, developer identity, project name, contact details and other agreed information.

Do you provide printing only or installation as well?

The scope is agreed for each project. You can request artwork and printing, production of individual panels or a broader solution with delivery and installation by arrangement.

Which materials are suitable for outdoor use in Cyprus?

Material selection depends on the surface, expected display period, fixing method and exposure. We consider sunlight, wind and the location of the perimeter when preparing a proposal.

Do you cover Paphos, Nicosia and Larnaca?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from Paphos, Nicosia, Larnaca, Limassol, Ayia Napa, Protaras, Paralimni and other areas across Cyprus. Site visits, delivery and installation are agreed for each location and project scope.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Please share the site location, fence type and approximate dimensions, number of surfaces, preferred graphics and whether installation is required. We will review the brief and prepare a project proposal.
