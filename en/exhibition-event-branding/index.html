05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you work outside Limassol?

Yes. NTEO accepts projects in Limassol, Nicosia, Paphos, Larnaca and other locations across Cyprus. Logistics and timing are coordinated for each venue.

Can we order individual elements only?

Yes. You can order a single exhibition stand, photo zone, stage backdrop, branded panels or event wayfinding.

Do you provide on-site installation?

Depending on the project, we can coordinate production, delivery and installation of structures and graphics at the event venue.
