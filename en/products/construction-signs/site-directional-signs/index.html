A clear brief makes a better result.

Where this solution is used

Construction Site Directional Signs can support identification, communication and the customer experience across retail, workplaces, hospitality and property environments. The right format depends on viewing distance, location, surface, lighting and the message that needs to be understood.

Design and material considerations

A successful specification starts with the installation environment. Consider whether the sign is indoors or outdoors, the expected viewing distance, exposure to sun and moisture, available fixing points, artwork dimensions and the finish that best fits the surrounding architecture.

What to prepare for a quotation

Share the approximate dimensions, installation address, photographs of the location, preferred wording or artwork, quantity and any deadline. If the final design is not ready, NTEO can first discuss the project requirements and suitable options.
