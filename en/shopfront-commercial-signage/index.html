07 / FAQ

QUESTIONS ABOUT SHOPFRONT AND COMMERCIAL SIGNAGE

Can I order dimensional letters without illumination?

Yes. We can discuss non-illuminated letters and logos in acrylic, metal, aluminium and other suitable materials.

What types of illuminated signs do you produce?

Options include internally illuminated letters, halo-lit lettering, lightboxes and illuminated panels. The construction depends on the dimensions, location and project requirements.

Can you brand a shop window without replacing the glass?

Yes. Window graphics can be applied using vinyl films, frosted and perforated films, decorative elements and information lettering.

Can you handle both the sign and the full shopfront?

Yes. Signage, façade panels, entrance features, window graphics and outdoor directional signs can be brought together into one visual concept.

Do you make illuminated menus and lightboxes?

Yes. We can discuss wall-mounted and projecting lightboxes, illuminated panels and lightboxes with replaceable printed graphics.

Which areas of Cyprus do you serve?

We welcome enquiries from Limassol, Paphos, Larnaca, Nicosia, Ayia Napa, Protaras, Paralimni and nearby towns. Delivery and installation are confirmed for each address.

COMMERCIAL SIGN MANUFACTURING AND SHOPFRONT BRANDING IN CYPRUS

NTEO provides commercial signage and shopfront branding in Cyprus for shops, boutiques, cafés, restaurants, salons, offices and other business premises. Depending on the brief, we produce illuminated signs, dimensional letters and logos, lightboxes, advertising panels, façade features and outdoor directional signs. For retail premises, we also create window graphics and entrance branding using printed and cut vinyl.

Interior branding is another part of the service. This can include reception desk finishes, wall logos, decorative panels, plaques and wayfinding elements. Materials are selected for the installation area and may include acrylic, aluminium, metal, composite panels, PVC, LED modules, vinyl films and printed graphics. Lighting, mounting, delivery and installation are discussed as part of the project quotation.
